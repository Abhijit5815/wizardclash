Wizard Clash

Your page, but with wizards.

Two wizards roam this page, dodge the text and fight with spells, shadow armies, giants and dragons. Everything you read here is a solid wall to them. Scroll fast and they will complain about it.

Add it to your page

Copy the src folder, then add four lines:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="src/wizardclash.css">
<script src="src/army-art.js"></script>
<script src="src/dragon-art.js"></script>
<script src="src/wizardclash.js" data-header=".top"></script>

That is all. No build step, no framework, no dependencies.

Choose what they dodge

By default headings, paragraphs, lists, images, buttons and anything with the class wizard-wall are solid. Pass your own list with data-walls=".card, h1, p" or WizardClash.start({ walls }).

Visitors stay in control

Two buttons sit in the corner. Petrify freezes everyone like statues. Silence switches the whole show off and remembers it. Anyone who prefers reduced motion never sees it at all.

A dragon on your cursor

Move the mouse and a dragon circles it. Double-click a spot and it breathes blue fire there.

Make it yours

The wizards, minions and dragons are plain vector data. Ask your coding agent to read AGENTS.md and swap in your own cast: a samurai and a ninja, rival footballers, your own team at work.
